# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes constructive contributions from readers, educators,
researchers, and LaTeX users of all experience levels.

Participants are expected to communicate respectfully, critique work rather
than people, give credit for contributions, protect private information, and
avoid discriminatory, intimidating, or harassing behaviour. Technical
disagreement is welcome when it remains evidence-based and collegial.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the
community. Concerns should be reported privately through the repository
owner's contact information rather than posted publicly.
