\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Comparing measurement methods requires clear definitions of the measured
quantity, units, pairing, and interpretation. Relevant terminology should be
checked against a current authoritative standard \parencite{iso5725}.

This article is a typesetting demonstration. The labels Method A and Method B
are neutral, and all values are \emph{simulated}.\footnote{The labels do not
name real devices, assays, observers, or clinical procedures.} The objective is
to describe the signed differences transparently, not to declare the methods
interchangeable.
