\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The small mean difference describes only this simulated set. It does not
validate a method, establish interchangeability, or estimate performance in a
population. A real study would prespecify an acceptable difference, justify its
sample, estimate uncertainty, and investigate patterns in the differences.

\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

A transparent comparison states the subtraction order, unit, pairing rule, and
limits of interpretation. Reproducible source and documentation support that
transparency but do not replace a valid study design.
