\chapter*{Common symbols}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Common symbols}

Symbols acquire meaning from a discipline and document; this guide gives \LaTeX{}
input and a common reading, not a universal definition. Define every symbol in
your work, distinguish variables from units, and consult the relevant style or
standard. Type the examples in mathematics mode unless stated otherwise.
