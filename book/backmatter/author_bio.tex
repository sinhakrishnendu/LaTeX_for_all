\chapter*{About the author}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{About the author}

Krishnendu Sinha, Ph.D., is an Associate Professor of Zoology at Siksha
Bhavana, Visva-Bharati, Santiniketan, West Bengal, India. He received his
Ph.D. in Molecular Medicine from Bose Institute, Kolkata, after completing an
M.Sc. at the University of Calcutta and a B.Sc. at Presidency College. Before
joining Visva-Bharati in 2026, he served in the West Bengal Education Service
at Jhargram Raj College.

His research combines molecular evolution with computational methods. His
interests include sequence analysis, adaptive molecular evolution,
phylogenetics, protein evolution, bioinformatics, and reproducible workflows.
He directs the Sinha Molecular Evolution Group and develops resources including
\mbox{BABAPPAlign} and \mbox{BABAPPASnake}.
