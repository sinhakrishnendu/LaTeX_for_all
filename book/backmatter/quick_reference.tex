\chapter*{Command quick-reference guide}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Command quick-reference guide}

This guide recalls patterns taught in the book. It is not a substitute for the
current class or package manual. Items shown as \placeholder{file} or
\placeholder{text} must be replaced; square brackets normally mark optional
arguments and braces required arguments.

\section*{Document skeleton and build}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.34\textwidth}p{.60\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Source or command & Purpose \\
\midrule
\endhead
\cmd{documentclass}\newline\optional{11pt}\required{article} & Select broad structure and supported options. \\
\cmd{usepackage}\newline\optional{options}\required{package} & Load one documented extension in the preamble. \\
\cmd{begin}\required{document} \ldots{} \cmd{end}\required{document} & Delimit visible document content. \\
\code{latexmk -pdf main.tex} & Build pdf\LaTeX{} dependencies and repeat passes automatically. \\
\code{latexmk -C} & Remove generated build files in the current project; use deliberately. \\
\code{texdoc geometry} & Open locally installed documentation for a named package. \\
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}

\section*{Labels and cross-references}

\begin{longtable}{@{}p{.34\textwidth}p{.60\textwidth}@{}}
\toprule
Pattern & Purpose \\
\midrule
\endhead
\cmd{label}\required{fig:growth} & Store the current numbered target under a unique semantic key. \\
\cmd{ref}\required{fig:growth} & Print only the target number. \\
\cmd{pageref}\required{fig:growth} & Print its page number. \\
\cmd{cref}\required{fig:growth} & Print type and number with \pkg{cleveref}. \\
\cmd{Cref}\required{fig:growth} & Capitalised form at the beginning of a sentence. \\
\cmd{eqref}\required{eq:model} & Parenthesised equation number with \pkg{amsmath}. \\
\cmd{autoref}\required{sec:methods} & Hyperlinked type and number with \pkg{hyperref}. \\
\bottomrule

Use consistent prefixes such as \code{chap:}, \code{sec:}, \code{fig:},
\code{tab:}, \code{eq:}, and \code{app:}. They aid navigation; \LaTeX{} does not
require those exact strings.

\section*{Release reminders}

Build from the documented main file; read the first meaningful error; search
for unresolved labels, citations, and placeholders; compile examples and
templates; inspect the log; render and inspect every final PDF page; verify
metadata and fonts where required; and archive the exact source, PDF,
supplements, manifest, permissions, and environment record.
