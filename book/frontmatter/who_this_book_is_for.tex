\chapter*{Who this book is for}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Who this book is for}

This book is for a complete beginner. You do not need experience with
programming, markup, command-line tools, or publishing. If words such as
\term{preamble}, \term{package}, and \term{compiler} are new, you are in the
right place: each will be introduced before it is required.

The examples are intended for students, teachers, researchers, laboratory and
field workers, data analysts, engineers, and technical professionals. They
draw from the biological, physical, chemical, mathematical, earth and
environmental, computer and data, engineering, technological, health, and
interdisciplinary sciences. You may skip examples outside your field without
losing the main argument.

Readers who already use \LaTeX{} can use the later chapters as a structured
course in maintainable scholarly documents. Optional sections marked
\emph{Advanced Topic} can be left until a project makes them useful.
