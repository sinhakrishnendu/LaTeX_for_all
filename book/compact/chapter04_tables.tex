\chapter{Tables That Readers Can Use}
\label{chap:tables}

\begin{learningoutcomes}
  \item Build a clean table with \pkg{booktabs}.
  \item Align numbers and wrap long text.
  \item Decide when a table should span pages.
\end{learningoutcomes}

\section{Start with a small professional table}

\begin{latexcode}{Complete example: a results table}
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{booktabs}
\usepackage{siunitx}

\begin{document}
\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{Simulated growth after seven days.}
  \label{tab:growth}
  \begin{tabular}{lS[table-format=2.1]S[table-format=2.1]}
    \toprule
    {Condition} & {Mean (mm)} & {SD (mm)} \\
    \midrule
    Control   & 12.4 & 1.8 \\
    Treatment & 16.7 & 2.1 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
\end{table}

Table~\ref{tab:growth} shows the larger mean in the
treatment condition.
\end{document}
\end{latexcode}

Use \code{l}, \code{c}, and \code{r} for ordinary columns. An \pkg{siunitx}
\code{S} column aligns numbers at the decimal marker. Put braces around a
textual heading above an \code{S} column.

\section{Wrap descriptive text}

\begin{latexcode}{Complete example: a table that fits the line}
\documentclass{article}
\usepackage{booktabs}
\usepackage{tabularx}

\begin{document}
\begin{table}[htbp]
  \centering
  \caption{A compact decision table.}
  \begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lXl@{}}
    \toprule
    Item & Check & Status \\
    \midrule
    Source & Commands are readable and commented. & Ready \\
    Figure & Labels remain legible at print size. & Revise \\
    PDF & Fonts are embedded and pages are correct. & Ready \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabularx}
\end{table}
\end{document}
\end{latexcode}

The \code{X} column takes the available width and wraps its content. The
\code{@\{\}} markers remove extra padding at the outer edges.

\section{Span several pages only when necessary}

\begin{latexcode}{Longtable pattern}
\usepackage{booktabs,longtable}

\begin{longtable}{@{}lp{0.62\linewidth}@{}}
\caption{Detailed checks.}\label{tab:checks}\\
\toprule
Item & Check \\
\midrule
\endfirsthead
\toprule
Item & Check \\
\midrule
\endhead
Source & Compile from a clean directory. \\
PDF & Inspect every page boundary. \\
% Add further rows here.
\bottomrule
\end{longtable}
\end{latexcode}

\pkg{longtable} is not a floating environment. It repeats the heading and
breaks between rows. Use it for genuinely long material, not for a table that
could be shortened.

\section{Repair the usual table errors}

\begin{center}
\begin{tabularx}{\linewidth}{@{}lX@{}}
\toprule
Symptom & Repair \\
\midrule
Extra alignment tab & Match the number of ampersands to the column definition. \\
Table runs into margin & Use \pkg{tabularx}, shorten headings, or redesign columns. \\
Numbers do not align & Use an \code{S} column instead of adding spaces. \\
Caption separated from table & Keep caption, tabular material, and label inside
one \code{table} environment. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabularx}
\end{center}

\begin{codelab}
Compile the results table. Add a third condition, change the numeric precision,
and write one sentence that refers to the table by label. Then build the
decision table using content from your own project.
\end{codelab}

\begin{chapterchecklist}
  \item The caption explains the table without repeating every value.
  \item Units appear in headings, not in every numeric cell.
  \item Decimal values align.
  \item The table fits without shrinking the main text.
\end{chapterchecklist}
