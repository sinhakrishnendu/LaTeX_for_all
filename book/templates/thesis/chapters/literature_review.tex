\chapter{Literature review}
\label{chap:literature}

A review should synthesise claims rather than arrange source summaries in a
list. For this demonstrator, the central methodological distinction is between
correlation and agreement. The typesetting system supports explicit document
structure and stable cross-references \parencite{lamport1994}, while literate
programming motivates keeping explanation close to reproducible computation
\parencite{knuth1984}.

A real review would document databases, search dates, inclusion criteria, and
the boundary between comprehensive and narrative coverage. Every factual claim
would be traced to a source actually read by the author.
