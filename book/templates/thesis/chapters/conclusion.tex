\chapter{Discussion and conclusion}
\label{chap:conclusion}

The simulated analysis illustrates a small mean offset, but it cannot support a
substantive claim about any real instrument. Interpretation would additionally
require a prespecified tolerance, uncertainty estimates, diagnostic checks,
and evidence that the sample represents the intended use population.

The demonstrator's contribution is structural: every claim can be followed to
a named method, equation, table, figure, or source. Future work should replace
the fixed values with a scripted workflow and add sensitivity analyses chosen
for the actual measurement process.
