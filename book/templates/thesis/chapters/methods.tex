\chapter{Methods}
\label{chap:methods}

Twenty paired observations were simulated for instructional use. No empirical
participants or confidential data were involved. For each pair,
\begin{equation}
  d_i=B_i-A_i,
  \label{eq:difference}
\end{equation}
and the mean difference was $\bar d=n^{-1}\sum_{i=1}^{n}d_i$. Illustrative
limits of agreement were $\bar d\pm1.96s_d$. The fixed values in this template
allow it to compile without external analysis software; a real thesis should
generate tables from a versioned analysis and record software versions.

The smallest scientifically important disagreement must be established before
interpreting the interval. A statistical interval alone cannot define
practical acceptability.
