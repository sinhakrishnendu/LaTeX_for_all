\chapter*{Executive summary}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Executive summary}

\textbf{Decision required.} Determine whether the simulated evidence is
sufficient to proceed to a controlled validation of measurement method B.

\textbf{Finding.} In 20 simulated pairs, method B exceeded method A by
\SI{0.18}{\milli\metre} on average. The teaching example does not define an
acceptable engineering tolerance and therefore cannot establish
interchangeability.

\textbf{Recommendation.} Proceed only to a prespecified validation stage. The
decision owner should approve the tolerance, sampling plan, calibration state,
and failure criteria before collecting empirical observations.

\textbf{Principal risk.} Treating high association as adequate agreement could
approve a method with operationally important systematic or dispersed error.
