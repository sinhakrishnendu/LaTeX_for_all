\chapter{Purpose, scope, and decision context}
\label{chap:context}

This report demonstrates how technical evidence can be organised around a
decision. It evaluates a simulated method-comparison data set; it does not
approve equipment, certify a process, or report observations from a real
organisation.

\section{Audience and use}
The primary audience is [decision owner], supported by [technical reviewers].
The report is intended to inform [named decision] by [decision date]. Any use
outside that scope requires reassessment of assumptions and evidence.

\section{Questions}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item What systematic difference appears between methods A and B?
  \item How variable are the paired differences?
  \item What additional evidence is needed before an operational decision?
\end{enumerate}

\section{Out of scope}
Hardware reliability, operator learning, environmental stress, cost, and
regulatory conformity are outside this teaching analysis.
