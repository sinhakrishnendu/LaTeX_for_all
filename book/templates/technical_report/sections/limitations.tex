\chapter{Limitations and assurance}

All observations in this template are simulated. The analysis omits repeated
measurements, operator effects, calibration drift, covariate-dependent error,
and uncertainty around agreement limits. No independent technical review has
occurred unless it is recorded in the document-control table.

Before issue, a reviewer should reproduce headline values, trace every finding
to evidence, test all links, inspect the final PDF, and confirm that the status
and classification shown on the cover and running header agree.
