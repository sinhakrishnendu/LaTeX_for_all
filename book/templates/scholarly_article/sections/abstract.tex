\begin{abstract}
We illustrate a reproducible comparison of two simulated measurement methods.
Twenty paired observations differed by a mean of \SI{0.18}{\milli\metre}, with
limits of agreement calculated as the mean difference plus or minus 1.96
standard deviations. This compact example demonstrates an abstract that states
the question, methods, principal result, and cautious interpretation without
citations or undefined abbreviations.
\end{abstract}

\noindent\textbf{Keywords:} measurement; agreement; reproducibility; simulated data
