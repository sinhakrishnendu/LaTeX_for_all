\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Agreement studies ask whether methods can be used interchangeably, not merely
whether their outputs are correlated. The distinction affects the analysis and
the claims that follow from it. Here we use a deliberately small simulated data
set to demonstrate a complete manuscript structure rather than to make an
empirical claim.

Scientific typesetting separates content from presentation
\parencite{knuth1984}. \LaTeX{} supports that separation by giving equations,
figures, tables, cross-references, and citations stable semantic structures
\parencite{lamport1994}. Our objective is to estimate the mean paired difference
between methods A and B and to report transparent agreement limits.
