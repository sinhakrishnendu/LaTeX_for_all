\section{Methods}
\label{sec:methods}

We generated 20 positive reference values and perturbed each value to create a
paired result from method B. The teaching data are simulated; no people,
animals, or confidential records are involved.

For pair $i$, the difference and pair mean were
\begin{align}
  d_i &= B_i-A_i, \label{eq:difference}\\
  m_i &= \frac{A_i+B_i}{2}. \label{eq:pair-mean}
\end{align}
The mean difference $\bar d$ estimates systematic offset. Illustrative
\SI{95}{\percent} limits of agreement were computed as
\begin{equation}
  \bar d \mathbin{\pm} 1.96s_d,
  \label{eq:limits}
\end{equation}
where $s_d$ is the sample standard deviation of the paired differences. All
calculations should be reproduced from the supplied analysis script in a real
submission; values here are fixed for a portable \LaTeX{} demonstration.
