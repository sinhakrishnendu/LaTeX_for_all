\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

The worked analysis suggests a small positive offset for method B in this
simulated sample. That observation does not establish interchangeability:
acceptable limits must be defined from scientific or clinical requirements,
and uncertainty around the limits should be considered.

The example is intentionally limited. Its sample is small, the distributional
assumptions are not stress-tested, and all values were constructed for
instruction. A real article would justify the sample size, document exclusions,
show diagnostic analyses, and connect every conclusion to the reported data.

In summary, the manuscript demonstrates a traceable chain from a stated
question through named equations to a table and figure. Replace the simulated
material rather than treating these values as evidence.
