# Full practical-edition outline

The edition retains the original spiral curriculum and adds a separate
**Complete Practical Coding** section after every chapter. Each practical
section contains a full runnable source file and a short modification task.

## Front matter

Title and copyright pages, family dedication, preface, audience, goals, usage
guide, **What this book covers---and what it does not**, conventions,
acknowledgements, and contents.

## Part I — Meeting LaTeX

- Chapter 0: source-to-PDF model and first complete file
- Chapter 1: first document and short report
- Chapter 2: commands, arguments, environments, comments, and errors

## Part II — Writing and Organising Text

- Chapter 3: readable scientific prose
- Chapter 4: article structure
- Chapter 5: lists, quotations, and notes

## Part III — Mathematics and Scientific Notation

- Chapter 6: essential mathematics
- Chapter 7: cases, matrices, theorems, and proofs
- Chapter 8: quantities, units, uncertainty, and numeric alignment

## Part IV — Tables, Figures, and Cross-References

- Chapter 9: publication-style numeric and text tables
- Chapter 10: aligned single and multi-panel figures
- Chapter 11: labels and automatic references

## Part V — Citations and Scholarly Sources

- Chapter 12: complete BibLaTeX/Biber file and citation patterns

## Part VI — Controlling a Professional Document

- Chapter 13: classes, packages, geometry, headers, and restrained design
- Chapter 14: complete multi-file book project

## Part VII — Major Scientific Document Forms

- Chapter 15: organised thesis driver
- Chapter 16: generic article and journal-conversion switch
- Chapter 17: evidence-to-recommendation technical report

## Part VIII — Working Efficiently and Safely

- Chapter 18: portable, reproducible project record
- Chapter 19: minimal diagnostic file and error commands
- Chapter 20: clean submission build and PDF preflight

## Part IX — Capstone Projects

- Chapter 21: single-file capstone integrating structure, mathematics, table,
  figure, references, bibliography, and final checks

## Back matter

Command reference, symbol guide, error guide, package guide, glossary,
solutions, verified bibliography, index, and author biography.

Part pages open on recto. Chapters use the next available page to keep the KDP
paperback economical and avoid unnecessary blank leaves.
