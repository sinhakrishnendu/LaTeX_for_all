\section{Complete Practical Coding}
\label{sec:practical-12}

The file creates its own demonstration bibliography database and runs with
the Bib\LaTeX{}--Biber workflow.

\begin{latexcode}{Complete practical file: bibliography and citations}
\begin{filecontents*}{practical-references.bib}
@article{example2026,
  author       = {Researcher, Ada and Researcher, Ben},
  title        = {A Demonstration Measurement Study},
  journaltitle = {Example Journal},
  year         = {2026},
  volume       = {1},
  pages        = {1--10}
}

@book{writing2025,
  author    = {Writer, Cara},
  title     = {A Demonstration Book Record},
  publisher = {Example Press},
  year      = {2025}
}
\end{filecontents*}

\documentclass{article}
\usepackage[backend=biber,style=authoryear]{biblatex}
\addbibresource{practical-references.bib}

\begin{document}

\textcite{example2026} demonstrates a narrative citation. A
parenthetical citation is also possible \parencite{writing2025}.

\printbibliography

\end{document}
\end{latexcode}

\begin{codelab}
Compile with \code{latexmk -pdf main.tex}. Replace the demonstration records
with checked records from your own sources, cite both keys, and confirm that
only cited works appear.
\end{codelab}
