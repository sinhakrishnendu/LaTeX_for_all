\section{Complete Practical Coding}
\label{sec:practical-04}

This pattern supplies the complete logical skeleton of a scholarly article.

\begin{latexcode}{Complete practical file: scholarly structure}
\documentclass{article}

\title{A Structured Demonstration Study}
\author{Your Name}
\date{}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
State the question, method, principal result, and conclusion.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
Define the problem and objective.

\section{Methods}
Describe materials, design, measurements, and analysis.

\section{Results}
Report the findings without interpretation.

\section{Discussion}
Interpret the findings and limitations.

\section{Conclusion}
Answer the objective directly.

\appendix
\section{Supplementary calculation}
Place supporting detail here.

\end{document}
\end{latexcode}

\begin{codelab}
Add a real title and objective, insert a subsection under Methods, and move the
appendix before the conclusion to observe how numbering responds.
\end{codelab}
