# Final PDF preflight

- Compile from a clean build and confirm the expected root file.
- Search the log for errors, undefined references, and overfull boxes.
- Inspect title pages, contents, headings, tables, figures, and final page.
- Confirm page size, margins, image resolution, and font embedding.
- Confirm metadata, page numbering, labels, captions, and bibliography.
- Keep the exact source and build instructions used for release.
